How It Works

From Vision to Installation

A clear, collaborative process — so you know exactly what to expect at every step.

  1. 01Consult

    We meet to understand your project — architecture, scale, species preference, and finish goals. We'll send samples.

  2. 02Design

    Our design team produces shop drawings for your review. Nothing goes into production until you sign off.

  3. 03Craft

    Your doors and moldings are milled, assembled, sanded, and finished in our Brooklyn facility.

  4. 04Deliver

    We package everything carefully and can arrange professional installation anywhere in the continental US.

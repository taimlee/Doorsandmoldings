Wainscoting Types

Four Complete Systems

Raised Panel

The most traditional wainscoting form — solid wood rails and stiles with raised center panels. Available in standard and custom panel configurations. Often paired with our traditional crown and base profiles.

Quoted per project
Flat Panel (Shaker)

A flat-centered panel in a solid wood frame — the shaker approach to wainscoting. Clean, architectural, and at home in transitional and contemporary interiors.

Quoted per project
Board & Batten

Wide vertical boards with narrow battens covering the seams — creates bold vertical rhythm. Farmhouse, coastal, and modern applications. Solid boards, not shiplap.

Quoted per project
Beadboard

Solid tongue-and-groove boards with a bead channel — classic cottage and Cape Cod wainscoting. Installs quickly and paints beautifully. Available in solid poplar and pine.

Quoted per project

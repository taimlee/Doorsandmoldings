Casing Profiles

Choose Your Style

Craftsman Flat

Clean flat stock with plinth and rosette corner blocks. The hallmark of Arts & Crafts and bungalow interiors.

Colonial Stop

A gentle curve with a stepped profile — traditional, versatile, and appropriate in formal and informal spaces alike.

Modern Flat

Dead-flat with a eased edge. The cleanest possible casing for modern and minimalist architecture.

Traditional Ogee

A reverse-curve ogee profile with backband. Rich and classical — at home in formal Georgian and Colonial Revival interiors.

Built-Up Pilaster

A multi-piece built-up casing that mimics a classical pilaster. Creates major visual weight around significant openings.

Any Profile + Backband

Add a backband to any casing profile to create depth and shadow — a quick way to add formality and weight.

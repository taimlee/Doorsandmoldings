Fire Ratings

Four Ratings. One Standard of Craft.

20
Minute Rating

Corridor and smoke-partition doors, including hotel guest-room and office suite entries off rated corridors.

45
Minute Rating

Openings in 1-hour fire barriers and fire partitions other than corridors.

60
Minute Rating

Openings in 1-hour exit stair and shaft enclosures.

90
Minute Rating

Openings in 2-hour walls, including exit stair and shaft enclosures in taller buildings.

Required ratings depend on the wall rating and your local code. Confirm with your architect or code official.
